Privacy Policy

This application is a personal analytics tool used solely by its owner to view their own social media account statistics (followers, views, video performance) over time.

The app connects to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts via each platform's official login. Data retrieved is stored privately and is used only to display it back to the account owner. No data is sold, shared, or made available to any third party.

The account owner may disconnect their accounts and request deletion of stored data at any time by contacting the app owner directly.
